# AtliQ Hardware – Power BI Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Power BI dashboard for AtliQ Hardware** designed to provide management with a clear view of sales, financial performance, and forecast accuracy.

The dashboard enables analysis across **customers, markets, products, segments, and channels**, with comparisons between current-year and previous-year performance.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive View

Provides a high-level overview of business performance through key KPIs and visualizations.

It covers **Net Sales, Gross Margin %, Net Profit %, Forecast Accuracy**, revenue by segment and channel, top customers and categories, and performance across markets and sub-zones.

### 2. Sales View

Focuses on sales and margin performance across different business dimensions.

It includes **customer sales and margin analysis, product sales and margin analysis, sales by channel, and market-level Gross Margin % vs Net Sales**.

### 3. Finance View

Provides a detailed view of the company's financial performance through a **Profit & Loss statement**.

It covers **Gross Sales, deductions, Net Sales, COGS, Gross Margin, Operational Expenses, Net Profit, and related percentage metrics**, along with market and segment-level analysis.

### 4. Forecast View

Analyzes the accuracy of sales forecasts and highlights potential inventory risks.

It compares **Sales Quantity vs Forecast Quantity**, calculates forecast errors and accuracy, and identifies risks such as **Out of Stock** and **Excess Inventory**.

## Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and visualization
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – Measures, KPIs, calculations, and time-based analysis
* **Data Modeling** – Relationships and analytical data model

## Key Features

* Interactive slicers for **region, market, customer, segment, category, and product**
* Current Year vs Previous Year comparison
* Dynamic KPI and metric analysis
* Sales and Gross Margin analysis
* Profit & Loss analysis
* Forecast Accuracy tracking
* Product and customer-level analysis
* Inventory risk identification
* Drill-down analysis across business dimensions
* Interactive dashboards for management-level performance review
